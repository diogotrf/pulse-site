Pulse

A clean daily tracker for Android: water, habits, gym sessions and weight.

  • Water — a daily goal, quick-add cups, and a recommended goal based on your weight and activity.
  • Habits — daily check-ins with streaks, a home-screen widget, and an optional reminder.
  • Gym — log sessions with sets, reps and weight, pre-filled from last time.
  • Weight — pick your weigh-in days and follow the trend.
  • History — charts and a month calendar of everything you logged.
  • Sync — optionally sign in with Google to keep your phones in sync through your own Google Drive.

Your data stays on your phone. If you turn on sync, it's stored in a private Pulse folder in your Google Drive — there are no Pulse servers, ads or tracking.
